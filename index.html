Background & roles

I was born and raised in Athens, Greece, and moved to the U.S. ten years ago. Now a U.S. citizen, I am completing my B.S. in Computer Engineering at the University of Maryland with a focus on ASIC/FPGA design and computer architecture. Throughout my tenure at UMD, I served as an Undergraduate Teaching Fellow for the circuits theory and lab course (ENEE205) and as a Technical Coordinator for an on-campus makerspace. In the latter role, I manage machine maintenance and ensure our end-to-end PCB fabrication remains fully operational to meet production deadlines.

In the fall, I will begin my Ph.D. at UMD as a Clark Doctoral Fellow, researching hardware security and multidimensional architectures under the guidance of Professor Srivastava. This summer, I am joining NVIDIA’s Santa Clara headquarters as an ASIC Floorplanning Intern. My long-term goal is to advance high-performance computing through novel architecture and robust backend implementation. Outside the lab, I follow motorsports closely and am currently training for my private pilot’s license.
